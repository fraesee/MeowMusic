# MeowMusic

A local music player built to look like an old forum. I did this for fun, whilst I do use it over spotify I don't expect it to take off or people to actually use it.<br>

## Controls:

**T** - Toggle Dark/Light mode<br>
**A** - Toggle Albums attachment<br>
**S** - Toggle Stats attachment<br>
**H** - Toggle History attachment<br>
**L** - Toggle Lyrics attachment<br>
**V** - Toggle Volume attachment<br>
**X** - Toggle Replay menu<br>

**P** - Pause/Unpause<br>
**R** - Restart track<br>
**Shift+R** - Random track<br>
**Shift+P** - Play selected album<br>
**Shift+L** - Toggle Loop<br>
**Spacebar** - Open song file explorer<br>

<br>**The directions are arrow keys**<br>

**Left** - Seek -5 seconds<br>
**Right** - Seek +5 seconds<br>
**Alt+Left** - Seek -10 seconds<br>
**Alt+Right** - Seek +10 seconds<br>

**Shift+Up** - Increase volume +1<br>
**Shift+Down** - Decrease volume -1<br>
**Up** - Increase volume +5<br>
**Down** - Decrease volume -5<br>
**Alt+Up** - Increase volume +10<br>
**Alt+Down** - Decrease volume -10<br>

**Shift+D** - Delete selected album<br>
